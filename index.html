First Session Recap: the teacher's eyes, in writing

Students say it best: "my teacher sees me." Today that feeling ends when Zoom closes. Here the teacher taps what they noticed, AI writes a warm recap and a 4-week path in the teacher's voice, and the student's weekly slot is held. The plan offer arrives attached to proof that someone saw them.

Drift Radar: notice the goodbye before the cancel click

People rarely quit yoga in a day. They drift for weeks: credits pile up, sessions get cancelled late, the weekly slot disappears. The Radar turns those quiet signals into one score and suggests the gentlest save, sent by the teacher first, because the relationship is the product. Switch the signals on and off.
